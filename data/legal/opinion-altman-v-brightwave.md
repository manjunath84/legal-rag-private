## Background
Plaintiff Dana Altman, a delivery driver, sued her former employer Brightwave Logistics,
Inc. for unpaid overtime under the state Wage and Hour Act. The trial court granted summary
judgment to Brightwave, holding that Altman was an independent contractor and therefore not
entitled to overtime. Altman appealed.

## Issue
Whether a worker who must follow the company's fixed routes, wear its uniform, and use its
handheld scanning device is properly classified as an employee rather than an independent
contractor for purposes of the Wage and Hour Act.

## Holding
We **reverse**. Applying the ABC test, we hold that Brightwave failed to establish prong B
because Altman's delivery work was within Brightwave's usual course of business. A worker is
presumed an employee unless the hiring entity proves all three prongs of the ABC test. The
degree of control Brightwave exercised — fixed routes, mandatory uniform, and company-issued
equipment — is inconsistent with independent-contractor status.

## Disposition
The judgment is reversed and the matter is remanded for further proceedings consistent with
this opinion. Costs to appellant.
