# MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (SYNTHETIC SAMPLE)

*For software testing only — not legal advice.*

This Master Services Agreement ("MSA") is made between **Atlas Cloud Services, Inc.**
("Provider") and **Riverside Health Group** ("Client"), effective **March 1, 2026**.

## 1. Services
Provider will perform the services described in one or more Statements of Work ("SOW")
executed under this MSA. Each SOW is governed by the terms of this MSA.

## 2. Fees and Payment
Client shall pay all undisputed invoices within **forty-five (45) days** of the invoice
date. Late amounts accrue interest at **1.0% per month** (or the maximum permitted by law,
if lower). Fees are exclusive of taxes.

## 3. Term and Renewal
This MSA has an initial term of **two (2) years** and renews automatically for successive
**one (1) year** periods unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least
**sixty (60) days** before the end of the then-current term.

## 4. Intellectual Property
Each party retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property. Provider assigns to
Client all right, title, and interest in the **custom deliverables** created specifically
for Client under a SOW, upon full payment for those deliverables.

## 5. Limitation of Liability
Except for breaches of confidentiality or a party's indemnification obligations, **each
party's total liability** under this MSA shall not exceed the **total fees paid by Client to
Provider in the twelve (12) months** preceding the event giving rise to the claim. Neither
party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages.

## 6. Confidentiality
Each party will protect the other's confidential information and use it only to perform
under this MSA. This obligation survives termination for **three (3) years**.

## 7. Governing Law
This MSA is governed by the laws of the **State of New York**, excluding its conflict-of-law
rules.
